About Vatli

Helping growing businesses run everything on WhatsApp.

Our mission

WhatsApp is where customers already are — with open rates most channels only dream of. Yet most businesses still run it from a single phone: messages get missed, there’s no shared history, and nothing is automated. Vatli exists to fix that. We turn WhatsApp into a professional platform where an entire team can sell, support, and grow from one number.

What we do

Vatli is a complete WhatsApp Business platform built on the official WhatsApp Business API. It brings together a shared team inbox, a lightweight CRM, sales pipelines, bulk broadcasts, message templates, no-code automations, visual chatbot flows, AI-assisted replies, and analytics — all in one clean, modern workspace that’s live in minutes.

Who we are

Vatli is a product of Ixoric Technologies LLC, a US-registered company. Ixoric operates the platform and handles secure billing and payments. Our small, focused team cares deeply about clean software, fair pricing, and genuinely helpful support.

Security & reliability

We take security seriously. Vatli runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, an enterprise-grade cloud platform. All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS), sensitive tokens are encrypted at rest, and every customer’s data is isolated with row-level security. We follow the principle of least privilege across our systems and are committed to GDPR and CCPA compliance. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

Our principles

  • Simple, honest pricing — clear plans, no hidden fees.
  • Privacy first — we never sell your data, and never use your customers’ messages for advertising.
  • Real support — friendly help when you need it, with setup guides included.
  • Compliance — we operate within Meta’s WhatsApp Business policies.
