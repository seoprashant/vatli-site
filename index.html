FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Vatli?

Vatli is a complete WhatsApp Business platform — a shared team inbox, CRM, broadcasts, automations, chatbot flows, AI agents, and analytics — built on Meta’s official WhatsApp Business API.

Do I need a WhatsApp Business API account?

Yes, and we make it easy. You connect your number through the official Facebook flow in about two minutes — no tokens to copy. We guide you through every step.

Is there a setup or onboarding cost?

No. Setup and onboarding are included at no extra cost — we help you configure templates, automations, and import contacts so you’re ready to go.

What does it cost to send messages?

Your Vatli subscription covers the software. Meta charges separately for WhatsApp conversations (rates vary by country and message type). Customer service replies within the 24-hour window are free.

Can my whole team use one number?

Absolutely. Add team members with roles (owner, admin, agent, viewer) and they all work from one shared inbox — no more sharing a single phone.

Can I keep using WhatsApp on my phone?

Yes — with Coexistence you can keep chatting from the WhatsApp Business app on your phone while your team uses Vatli on the same number.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We run on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest, per-tenant data isolation, and role-based access. We are GDPR and CCPA compliant and never sell your data.

Do you offer support?

Yes — 24/7 technical support over WhatsApp and email, plus setup guides and training. Priority support is included on the Business plan.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month or yearly and you can cancel at any time; cancellation simply stops future renewals.

Is there an API for developers?

Yes. Vatli includes a full REST API and webhooks so you can integrate WhatsApp with your website, store, or internal systems.
